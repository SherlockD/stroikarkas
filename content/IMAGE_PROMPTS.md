# Промпты демонстрационных изображений

Все девять изображений сгенерированы встроенным `image_gen` в режиме `photorealistic-natural`, затем проверены и оптимизированы в WebP.

## СКТ–65

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: website project catalog card cover
Primary request: create a photorealistic architectural photograph of a compact single-storey timber-frame garden house with a welcoming covered terrace and restrained dark-green accents.
Scene/backdrop: a tidy but natural residential plot in Mari El / central Russia, with mixed birch and pine trees, modest lawn and native shrubs; believable rural surroundings.
Subject: one compact, buildable rectangular timber-frame house; warm natural-wood horizontal cladding; simple pitched roof; covered wooden terrace; dark-green front door, window frames and small facade trim accents; realistic modern windows and proportions.
Style/medium: professional real-estate and architectural photography, genuinely photorealistic, natural material texture and slight real-world imperfections, not a CGI render.
Composition/framing: horizontal 4:3 composition; eye-level three-quarter exterior view; show the entire house and terrace with comfortable margins; house is the clear focal point and remains suitable for a centered website-card crop; straight vertical lines, natural perspective.
Lighting/mood: soft natural daytime light under lightly diffused clouds, realistic shadows, fresh calm atmosphere, neutral color balance.
Materials/textures: visible timber grain, believable joinery, matte dark-green painted wood/metal details, realistic roof surface, natural grass and soil.
Constraints: exactly one main house; no people; no animals; no cars; no construction equipment; no signs; no house numbers; no text; no logos; no watermarks.
Avoid: luxury villa scale; fantasy architecture; impossible geometry; fisheye distortion; oversaturated colors; excessive HDR; glossy 3D-render appearance.
```
